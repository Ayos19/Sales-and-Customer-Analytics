# Sales Performance & Customer Demographics Dashboard
## Dashboard Overview

The Sales Performance & Customer Demographics Dashboard provides an interactive analysis of sales performance, customer demographics, product categories, customer reviews, and order locations.

The dashboard is designed to help businesses understand purchasing patterns, evaluate product performance, monitor sales trends, and gain insights into customer behaviour. It provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs), charts, and interactive filters.

## Tools and Methods

- **Microsoft Excel:** Used for data preparation, analysis, KPI calculations, and dashboard development.
- **Data Cleaning:** Preparation and organization of sales and customer records.
- **Data Analysis:** Evaluation of customer demographics, product categories, sales performance, and customer reviews.
- **Data Visualization:** Charts, graphs, and KPI indicators used to communicate business insights.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Filters and visual elements designed to support dynamic data exploration.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Product Performance:** Prioritize inventory planning and marketing strategies around clothing, which contributes the largest share of total sales.

2. **Customer Engagement:** Develop targeted marketing strategies that consider the purchasing patterns of male and female customers.

3. **Customer Satisfaction:** Investigate negative customer reviews to identify opportunities to improve product quality and customer experience.

4. **Geographical Sales Strategy:** Evaluate high-performing order locations to identify opportunities for business expansion and improved sales distribution.

5. **Shipping Cost Optimization:** Review shipping fees across locations to identify opportunities for more efficient order fulfilment.

6. **Sales Growth:** Monitor monthly and quarterly sales performance to identify trends and support better business planning.

---

## Limitations

- The dashboard provides historical sales analysis rather than real-time sales monitoring.
- The available visualization does not establish the reasons behind changes in sales performance.
- Customer purchasing behaviour is analysed through available demographic and product category information.
- Further analysis would be required to establish the relationship between shipping costs, customer satisfaction, and repeat purchases.

---

This dashboard demonstrates how data analysis and visualization can transform sales and customer information into meaningful business insights, supporting informed decision-making, improved customer engagement, and better sales performance management.
